InSync is a real-time collaborative music streaming application that allows friends to listen to music together remotely, similar to a shared radio room. Users can create or join listening rooms where music playback stays synchronized across all participants. When the host plays, pauses, or skips a song, the change is reflected instantly for everyone in the room. The platform focuses on recreating the experience of friends listening to music together, even when they are physically apart.
